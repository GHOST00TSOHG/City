# References

Links only. No third-party images are stored in this folder. The mesh is original work measured from these sources.

## Footprint and placement

- OpenStreetMap way 166526695, Clark's Pond. © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL. https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/166526695
- OpenStreetMap way 166526759, the water body north of Clark's Pond (the Midwood pool side of the brook). © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL. https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/166526759
- OpenStreetMap way 1257648717, the footway that crosses that corridor. © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL. https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/1257648717

The bridge itself is not a separate tagged way. The model origin is where way 1257648717 crosses the line from the north tip of Clark's Pond to the south edge of way 166526759. The county report places the Laurel Wood Boulder Bridge on the brook between Clark's Pond and Midwood Pool, directly north of the pond inlet, with the path running across the brook.

## Dimensions

- Branch Brook Park Alliance, Cultural Landscape Report, Volume 4: Structures in the Park (January 2005), section 19, Laurel Wood Boulder Bridge. Published report. https://branchbrookpark.org/uploads/1/3/1/7/131747681/bbp_clr_vol4_1_-_structures_in_the_park.pdf

The report gives the bridge as about 8 ft wide and 15 ft long, with coping boulders about 3 ft across, fieldstones about 12 in, and an arched concrete culvert. Cheek walls on the small brook bridges of this family stand about 2 ft 4 in above the deck. Short earth ramps are added so the deck meets the path.

No Google imagery was used. No cherry trees are in this mesh.
