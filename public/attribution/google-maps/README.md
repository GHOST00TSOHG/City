The SVGs are byte-for-byte copies. Do not edit, recolor, crop or stretch them.

In use: `GoogleMaps_Logo_WithDarkOutline.svg` (an outlined logo, because it sits over the busy map),
shown at its native 105×22 px. The logo glyph inside is 18 px tall (allowed range 16-19).
Other variants (light outline, white, gray, dark gray) are kept for other backgrounds.
