# References

## Dimensions

- National Register of Historic Places registration, Wars of America, Newark, reference 94001257. Public domain. The monument on its granite coping is 42 ft long, 18 ft wide and 17 ft high. Forty-two figures and two horses. https://npgallery.nps.gov/GetAsset/c9ba4252-6f30-4db4-999a-d7f8889d53bf
- Wikipedia summary of the same sculpture, including the Stone Mountain granite base and Borglum's spearhead description. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wars_of_America
