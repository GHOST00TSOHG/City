# References

Links only. No third-party images are stored in this folder. The mesh is original work measured from these sources.

## Footprint and placement

- OpenStreetMap way 511381537, the one-storey building at 55 Park Place inside Military Park. © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL. The way is tagged with the current restaurant tenant. This model does not use that name. https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/511381537
- OpenStreetMap node 4719897387, Wars of America, used only to decide which long wall faces the park. https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/4719897387

## What the building is

- H3, Military Park, Newark. 2014 restoration: a comfort station and restaurant at the eastern end of the plaza. The sword in front of Wars of America is the old reflecting pool, now planted. https://www.h3hc.com/portfolio/newark-military-park/
- Let It Grow, Military Park. 2013–2014 work included the restaurant structure. https://letitgrow.com/project/military-park/
- Newark City Parks Foundation. The park is the public lawn bounded by Broad Street, Park Place and Rector Street. https://www.newarkcityparks.org/military-park

OSM has no height. The mesh is one storey, 3.55 m to the wall and 3.86 m over the roof, judged against the 17 ft monument in the 2020 photographs below.
