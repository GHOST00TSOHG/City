# References

Links only. No third-party images are stored in this folder. The mesh is original work measured from these sources.

## Footprint and placement

- OpenStreetMap relation 5506232, Cathedral Basilica of the Sacred Heart. Outer ring way 370622894, courtyard hole way 370622986. © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL. https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/5506232
- The outer ring also takes in a north annex. The model origin is the centre of the 365 ft church, measured from the west tower buttress tips on that ring along the building axis. It is not the centroid of the whole ring.

## Dimensions

- National Register of Historic Places nomination, Cathedral of the Sacred Heart, Newark, reference 76001151. Public domain. https://npgallery.nps.gov/NRHP/GetAsset/b5fa0ed7-4a53-4132-acfe-a572de0d7a57
- Structurae, overall height 79 m and tower height 71 m (the tower figure matches the nomination's 232 ft). https://structurae.net/en/structures/cathedral-basilica-of-the-sacred-heart
- Institute for Sacred Architecture, materials note (granite, limestone, brownstone foundation, copper fleche, Lady Chapel and radiating chapels). https://www.sacredarchitecture.org/articles/gem_among_smokestacks
