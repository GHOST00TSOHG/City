# References

Links only. No third-party images are stored in this folder. The mesh is original work measured from these sources.

## Footprint and placement

- OpenStreetMap way 1257648454, `amenity=shelter`, `building=yes`, `shelter_type=picnic_shelter`, on Meeker Mound in Branch Brook Park. © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL. https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/1257648454
- The way is a loose 21-point ring, about twice the measured building. The manifest uses the polygon centroid of that ring. It does not use the ring as the footprint.

## Dimensions and materials

- Cultural Landscape Report, Treatment, and Management Plan for Branch Brook Park, Newark, New Jersey. Volume 4: Structures in the Park, January 2005, section 13, "The Octagon Shelter". Prepared for the Branch Brook Park Alliance and the Essex County Department of Parks, Recreation and Cultural Affairs. https://branchbrookpark.org/uploads/1/3/1/7/131747681/bbp_clr_vol4_1_-_structures_in_the_park.pdf
- The report's field measure is about 13 ft 10 in on each side, about 33 ft 9 in across, and about 22 ft 6 in high. This mesh uses the side length as a regular octagon (flat-to-flat 33.4 ft). The 4 inch gap is the report's "approximately".
- Essex County Parks, Branch Brook Park. https://essexcountyparks.org/parks/branch-brook-park/about
