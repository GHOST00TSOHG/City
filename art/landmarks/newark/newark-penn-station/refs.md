# References

Links only. No third-party images are stored in this folder. The mesh is original work measured from these sources.

## Footprint and placement

- OpenStreetMap way 49200094, Newark Penn Station. © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL. https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/49200094
- The way is the station complex, about 366 m along the tracks. That matches the National Register's 1,200 ft train shed. The west wall of the station house is the straight run at the northwest edge of the polygon, 92.2 m long.
- Market Street and Raymond Boulevard crossings are the OSM highway ways that pass under that polygon. The street arches are placed on those crossings. Market Street meets the shed on a diagonal, so the west and east mouths of that passage are shifted along the shed.

## Dimensions

- National Register of Historic Places nomination, Pennsylvania Station, Newark, reference 78001760. Public domain. Station house 302 ft by 79 ft. Waiting room ceiling 46 ft. Train shed 1,200 ft, extending about 300 ft north and 600 ft south of the house, over the eastern three-fourths of the house. https://npgallery.nps.gov/NRHP/GetAsset/c0b4ec8b-58ac-4b7d-909a-040d3dcf7d4c

West facade in an a-b-c-b-a rhythm, pink granite base, Indiana limestone body, twin stilted arches, pediments, north and south end windows, buff brick shed with limestone surrounds, green terra cotta, segmental street arches. https://npgallery.nps.gov/GetAsset/3afc9307-17be-44c4-b1fa-ab7ae7d0c947
