# References

Links only. No third-party images are stored in this folder. The mesh is original work measured from these sources. The photographs were used to check massing. They were not traced.

- OpenStreetMap way 1049904856, Lake Street. © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL. https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/1049904856
- OpenStreetMap way 11622434, Ballantine Parkway. © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL. https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/11622434

The origin is the shared corner of Lake Street and Ballantine Parkway, 40.771081, -74.173518. The street face looks back down the parkway toward that corner.

## Dimensions

- National Register of Historic Places nomination, Branch Brook Park, November 1980. Public domain. The gate is 11 ft high and 40 ft wide, between two one-story red-brick and limestone pavilions. https://branchbrookpark.org/uploads/1/3/1/7/131747681/essex_county_branch_brook_park_-_national_registrar_of_historic_places_inventory_nomination_form_nov1980.pdf
- Branch Brook Park Alliance, Cultural Landscape Report, Volume 4 part 2 (January 2005), section 25, The Ballantine Gate. Pedestrian flat arches 5 ft by 8 ft 6 in, granite plinth about 21 in, limestone base cap 16 in, 13 in belt and fascia, rafters 4 ft past the wall, hip roofs with a kick at the eave. https://branchbrookpark.org/uploads/1/3/1/7/131747681/bbp_clr_vol4_2_-_structures_in_the_park.pdf
